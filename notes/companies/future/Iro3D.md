Small company run by a DIY person. Offers two printers, or more like powder pourer's. Does not include a furnace. One is medium volume and costs 10000$ and the other is large volume and costs 30000$.
The website has great resources regarding the considerations that go into SPD. For example oxidation, and what atmosphere should be used to sinter a part.
