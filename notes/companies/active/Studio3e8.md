## Initial email
"Dear Casey and Cassie,

I am a research assistant at the Technical University of Denmark (DTU), working with our magnetics research group on additive manufacturing of soft-magnetic components for power electronics.

Our research focuses on developing soft-magnetic cores with multiple permeability regions by using additive manufacturing to combine materials with different magnetic properties within a single component. Longer term, we are also interested in integrating conductor windings.

We would be interested in acquiring a Mesa 1 for research purposes and exploring the possibility of an early-access research evaluation or pilot collaboration. An important consideration for us would be the flexibility to use third-party powders and develop or modify material-specific deposition and sintering parameters. Could you clarify to what extent the platform supports this?

We are offering a mutually beneficial collaboration by testing suitable soft-magnetic powder feedstocks and exploring multi-material deposition and co-sintering. Through systematic test printing and research-level characterization at DTU, including magnetic, electrical, and microstructural measurements, we could provide your team with experimental data, identify process limitations, and offer iterative feedback to help optimize the platform and explore new material applications.

The research could also potentially lead to peer-reviewed publications, subject to mutual agreement, with appropriate acknowledgement of the machine and collaboration.

Would this be of interest to your team? If so, we would be happy to arrange a short online meeting to discuss the possibilities further. In the meantime, any available technical information, particularly regarding sintering conditions and multi-material capabilities, would be greatly appreciated.

Thank you for your time. I look forward to hearing from you.

Sincerely,
Artis Fils"
