KERAFOL Keramische Folien GmbH & Co. KG makes thermal management materials: KERATHERM (tapes, pads, liquids) and KERAMOLD, a silicone-free, thermally conductive TPE granulate for injection molding and overmolding. It's electrically insulating except for HIGHCARB, which is electrically conductive. They also offer injection molding and prototyping as a service, 3D-printed molds, and **granulate-fed 3D printing of KERAMOLD**, which could be relevant for pellet extrusion.
