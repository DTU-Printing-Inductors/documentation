Structural carbon-fibre feedstocks that are conductive as a side effect; most FGF pellets fall here. Printing raises ρ by up to about three decades compared with molding.

## Sources
- PA/CF composites for FFF, *Journal of Materials Engineering and Performance* (2021), [10.1007/s11665-021-05635-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11665-021-05635-1): compression-molded 13-15 Ω·cm, FFF-printed 0.7-1.7e4 Ω·cm (-16 to 100 °C).
- Continuous carbon fibre EMI shielding study, *Polymers* (2023), [PMC10748082](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10748082/): EMI shielding needs roughly ≥5 S/m (≤20 Ω·cm); continuous CF 1-23 Ω·cm, printed Onyx far more resistive.
- Avient Stat-Tech CF compounds (datasheets): about 1e2-1e4 Ω·cm, molded, injection-molding grades. [SpecialChem](https://www.specialchem.com/plastics/product/avient-formerly-polyone-stat-tech-as-15cf-000)
- Your own MOCOM / Envalior CF notes in notes/hardware/materials can refine this range.
