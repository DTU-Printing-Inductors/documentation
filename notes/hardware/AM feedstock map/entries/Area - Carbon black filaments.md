Range = measured **filament** resistivity of five commercial carbon-black filaments in Nowka et al. 2024 (6.53-48.18 Ω·cm). Printed parts are higher: XY parallel 11.7-172.5 Ω·cm, Z 8.0-365.9 Ω·cm.

## Sources
- Nowka, Ruge, Schulze, Hilbig & Vietor, *Polymers* 16 (2024) 2891, [10.3390/polym16202891](https://doi.org/10.3390/polym16202891): Amolen 22.33, Conductive Filaflex 9.10, Protopasta 6.53, 3dkonductive 48.18, Eel 10.63 Ω·cm (filament); printed values per orientation in Table 4.
- Stankevich et al., *Polymers* 15 (2023) 2988, [10.3390/polym15142988](https://doi.org/10.3390/polym15142988): Proto-pasta filament 3.13 Ω·cm (newer coil 5.1), printed monolayers 8.2-15 Ω·cm.
- Flowers, Reyes, Ye, Kim & Wiley, *Additive Manufacturing* 18 (2017) 156-163, [Duke record](https://scholars.duke.edu/publication/1289396): printed carbon-black traces 12 Ω·cm.
- FILI conductor (graphite, 220 Ω·cm filament) is excluded from this range; see Nowka Table 4.
