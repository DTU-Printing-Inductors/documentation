Pure-copper material extrusion (pellet, filament, paste routes) after debinding and sintering: 51.7-86.1 % IACS = 3.3e-8 to 2.0e-8 Ω·m. Only reached after sintering (~1000 °C) with shrinkage.

## Sources
- Sakib-Uz-Zaman & Khondoker, *Metals* 13 (2023) 859, [10.3390/met13050859](https://doi.org/10.3390/met13050859): review of nine pure-Cu MEX studies, 51.7-86.1 % IACS.
- Electrical conductivity upon sintering of pure Cu produced by MEX, *J. Manufacturing Processes* (2024), [link](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1526612524002640): pore, grain size and impurity effects.
- ORNL, 3D printing + HIP of copper from MIM feedstock: about 86 % to 100 % IACS after HIP. [link](https://impact.ornl.gov/en/publications/advancements-in-3d-printing-and-hot-isostatic-pressing-of-copper-/)
- IACS: 100 % = 58.0 MS/m = 1.7241e-8 Ω·m.
