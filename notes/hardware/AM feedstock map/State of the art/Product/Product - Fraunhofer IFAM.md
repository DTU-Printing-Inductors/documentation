Up to 1e6 S/m (1e-6 Ω·m) is the maximum Fraunhofer IFAM states for its compounds, not a value tied to one printed part. Counted as printed because IFAM makes these compounds into FFF filament.

## Sources
- [Electrically conductive plastics](https://www.ifam.fraunhofer.de/en/technologies/electrically-conductive-composites-.html): filler contents around 90 wt%, conductivity up to 10^6 S/m, up to 20 A; processed by injection moulding, extrusion and FFF filament. IFAM is a development partner and service provider, not a seller.
- [Brochure "Elektrisch und thermisch leitfähige Polymerkomposite"](https://www.ifam.fraunhofer.de/content/dam/ifam/de/documents/Formgebung_Funktionswerkstoffe/Funktionsstrukturen/elektrisch-und-thermisch-leitfaehige-polymerkomposite.pdf): fillers can be solid and molten metals ("feste und schmelzflüssige metallische Substanzen") or ceramic powders, up to 50 vol%; matrices PA6, PA6.6, PA12, PP, PPS, ABS and TPE; shielding 80-90 dB at 300 kHz-1.2 GHz.
