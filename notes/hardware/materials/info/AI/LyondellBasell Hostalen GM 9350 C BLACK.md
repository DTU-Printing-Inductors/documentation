ALBIS/M-Base data sheet. Black HDPE, delivered as black pellets. Volume resistivity 1E5 Ω·m and surface resistivity 1E4 Ω (TDS labels it resistivity but gives the unit as Ω, so it's recorded under surface resistance). Density 995 kg/m³, bulk density 550 kg/m³, MFI 3 g/10 min (190 °C, 21.6 kg). Sibling grade of [[LyondellBasell Hostalen GM 9310 C BLACK]].
