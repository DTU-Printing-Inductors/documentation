From LATI's LatiLambda material page. High thermal conductivity product based on Polyamide 6 (PA 6). Special filler. Flame retardant, UL94 V-0 class, free of halogens-based flame retardants and red phosphorous. High stiffness. PFAS-free product. Surface resistivity given as 1E12 ohm (IEC 62631), so it's recorded under surface resistance. Density 1.77 g/cm³. Flammability: 0.75 mm thickness: V-2, 1 mm thickness: 960, 1.5 mm thickness: V-0, 2 mm thickness: 960, 3 mm thickness: V-0.
