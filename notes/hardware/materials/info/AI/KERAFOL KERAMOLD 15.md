From the KERAMOLD catalogue (2027-10). Colour orange. Thermal conductivity λ 1.5 W/mK and thermal resistance 1.66 K/W, measured at 1.0 mm thickness. The catalogue doesn't say whether λ is integral or through-plane (a later page mentions "through plane" for the series). Electrically insulating: breakdown voltage > 16 kV AC, CTI 600 V. Hardness 65-80 Shore A, density 1.9 g/cm³, application temperature -40 to +125 °C, Tg -47 °C. KERAFOL also offers granulate-fed 3D printing of KERAMOLD.
