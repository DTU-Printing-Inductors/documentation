From the KERAMOLD catalogue (2027-10). Colour grey. Thermal conductivity λ 2.0 W/mK and thermal resistance 1.25 K/W, measured at 1.0 mm thickness. The catalogue doesn't say whether λ is integral or through-plane. Electrically insulating: breakdown voltage 3 kV AC, CTI 600 V. Very soft: 15-30 Shore A, density 1.91 g/cm³, application temperature -40 to +100 °C, Tg -59 °C. This is the grade used in both catalogue case studies (BMS overmolding: PCB at 45 °C vs 90 °C bare; e-motor overmolding beat potting). Nearly identical to [[KERAFOL KERAMOLD 20N]] apart from hardness and breakdown voltage.
