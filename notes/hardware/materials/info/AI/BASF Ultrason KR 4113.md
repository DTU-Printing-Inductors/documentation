ALBIS/M-Base data sheet (ISO 1043: PESU+PTFE-(CF+CD)30). PES compound whose filler system (carbon fibres, graphite, PTFE powder) is designed for tribological performance, with low thermal expansion and low swelling in hot oils. Electrical values are conditioned only: volume resistivity - / 8500 Ω·m, surface resistivity * / 4000 Ω. The TDS labels this "Surface Resistivity" but gives the unit as Ω, so it's recorded under surface resistance. Density 1500 kg/m³, Tg 225 °C, HDT 222 °C (1.8 MPa). Very high processing temperatures (350-390 °C melt).
