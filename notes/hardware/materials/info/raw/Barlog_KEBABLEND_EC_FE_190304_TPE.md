## Brief description of the product family:

KEBABLEND is a wide range of functional compounds, often tailor-made to customer requirements. Under the trade name KEBABLEND, we market magnetizable, thermally or electrically conductive compounds, high-density injection molding materials, compounds for radiation protection applications, detectable plastics

and much more.

## Properties:

antistatic, electrically conductive, flexible, good haptics, semi-crystalline, soft, PFAS-free

## Processing instructions:

## Pre-drying:

Dryer type: dry air dryer

Temperature: 80°C

Drying time: 2-4 h

## Temperatures:

Melt temperature: 230 - 270°C

Mold temperature: 20 - 60°C

## Dwell time on the machine:

Aim for short dwell time, empty cylinder when production is interrupted, lower temperature, flush with fresh material when restarting.

Injection speed and injection pressure depend on the component. Too high speed and shear can worsen conductivity.
