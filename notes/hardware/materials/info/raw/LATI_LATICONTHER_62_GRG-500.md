## General information

High thermal conductivity product based on Polyamide 6 (PA 6). Graphite / Glass fibres. PFAS-free product.

The products mentioned herein are not suitable for applications in contact with foodstuffs or for potable water transportation, or for toy manufacturing.

The products mentioned herein are not suitable for applications in the pharmaceutical, medical or dental sector.
