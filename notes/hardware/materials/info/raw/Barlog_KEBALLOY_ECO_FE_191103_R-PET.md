## Technical data KEBALLOY ECO FE 191103 R-PET (Development product*)

KEBALLOY ECO FE 191103 PET is an electrically conductive PET compound with glass and carbon fiber reinforcement. The PET base polymer is made from post-consumer recycled PET bottles and the carbon fibers are post-industrial recycled. KEBALLOY ECO FE 191103 PET is thus a sustainable alternative to other electrically conductive structural materials.

## Brief description of the product family:

KEBALLOY ECO is our product family of engineering plastics and high-performance compounds based on post-consumer and post-industrial recyclates.

The portfolio enables manufacturers to integrate recycled content into technical applications while maintaining the performance characteristics required for their specific use case. Depending on the material selection and application, KEBALLOY ECO compounds can contribute to reduced product carbon footprint values compared to alternative virgin material solutions.

In addition, KEBALLOY ECO supports customer-specific recycling and take-back concepts, helping companies implement circular material strategies and make greater use of existing material resources.

## Properties:

dimensionally stable, electrically conductive, good chemical resistance, good aging behavior, high continuous used temperature, High strength, High stiffness, semi-crystalline, PFAS-free

Fasteners, Hardware, Housing, Sliding elements, Bearing bushes, Structural components

## Processing instructions: Pre-drying:

Dryer type: dry air dryer

Temperature: 80°C

Drying time: 4-8 h

Moisture content during processing: &lt; 0.02%.

## Recommended basic settings:

Melt temperature: 260 - 290°C

Mold temperature: 140 - 160 °C

Injection speed: high

Back pressure: 40 - 80bar (spec.)

## Machine selection:

Screw: 3-zone screw with non-return valve

Nozzle: Open nozzle or shut-off nozzle

Wear protection: Wear protected according to machine manufacturer's recommendation, suitable for

processing fiber reinforced plastics

Injection unit: Shot volume = 50-80% of the maximum metering volume
