## Sources to look into

https://queenspowergroup.com/Pub/Papers/2012/2012_ECCE_L_Wang_Multi_Perm_Air_Gap_Inductor.pdf
The authors formulate an ideal permeability profile that increases from the inner to the outer region of a core, aiming to make better use of the magnetic material and improve inductance. They explicitly note that a continuously varying profile is difficult to fabricate and therefore approximate it with discrete regions. They experimentally compare a three-permeability inductor with a uniform-permeability design.

https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11837-018-2857-5
This paper demonstrates spatially varying permeability in an actual inductor core, manufacturing with traditional methods.
