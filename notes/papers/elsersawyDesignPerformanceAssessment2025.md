## Interpretation
> [!PDF|red] [[s41598-025-19740-x-3.pdf#page=1&selection=45,74,53,56&color=red|s41598-025-19740-x-3, p.1]]
> > Results showed that the Split Path and Full Turn Helix mixers provided the most uniform color distribution, with minimal segregation. In contrast, the Helix Array, Half Moon, and Cross Bars designs produced moderate to inconsistent mixing, showing visible streaking and uneven blending. All mixer configurations, however, significantly outperformed the control (no mixer) setup.
> 
> Results show that mixing uniformity depends on the mixing geometry.
