> > The transition between materials was found to be directionally dependent, with ABS to CF/ABS having a transition length of 3.5 m compared to 3.2 m for CF/ABS to ABS. Furthermore, the transition from Material A to Material B was found to be repeatable with a possible variance in transition length of 0.3 m.
> 
> Transitions between material compositions occupy a finite distance, or basically when switching there's a delay.
