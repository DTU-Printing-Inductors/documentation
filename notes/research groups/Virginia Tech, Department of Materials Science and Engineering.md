2006 They first formulated the nanoscale silver paste, "Low-Temperature Sintered Nanoscale Silver as a Novel Semiconductor Device-Metallized Substrate Interconnect Material"

After some years, the group moved from screen printing to AM, and the primary author became Yi Yan as a PhD student under supervision of Guo-Quan Lu. 2016 The first AM magnetic paper is titled "Additive manufacturing of magnetic components for power electronics integration"[[yiyanAdditiveManufacturingMagnetic2016]]

2017 Then her PhD was published titled "Design methodology and materials for additive manufacturing of magnetic components", it could be very beneficial to read.

2017 Then the group attempted to changed the material to NiCuZn ferrite paste to achieve higher permeability, and they encountered slumping therefore they wanted to solidify as it was printed and the resulting paper is titled "UV-assisted 3D-printing of Soft Ferrite Magnetic Components for Power Electronics Integration". Higher sintering temperatures and UV-assisted solidification.

2017 Then they basically republished the same paper from before with more information titled "Additive Manufacturing of Magnetic Components for Heterogeneous Integration"

2017 As previously only planar inductor was attempted, they decide to try a toroidal, where the winding is printed around a magnetic core. [[yanAdditiveManufacturingToroid2017]]

2017 Then group investigated gelcasting at different sintering temperatures, titled "NiCuZn Ferrite Cores by Gelcasting: Processing and Properties"

2017 As the sintering temperatures remained 250 for the iron filled paste and 950 for the NiCuZn there were attempts at decreasing the temperature, and they investigated material properties of molded paste at 250 still. "Design, Fabrication, and Characterization of a Low-Temperature Curable Magnetic Composite for Power Electronics Integration"

2018 Then they attempted multipermeability [liuDesignAdditiveManufacturing2018]

Eventually Yi Yan got hired by Texas instruments and then she started filing patents.

The research continued by Guo-Quan Lu. Notable research papers are
